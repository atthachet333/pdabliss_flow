## Behind the Product (specification for a later episode)

Sections: **Why FLOW** (everyday coordination across family and company without exposing the
wrong details to the wrong people) · **What it solves** (right time, right people, right group) ·
**Design philosophy** (editorial calm, Thai-first legibility, Sally's restraint) · **Engineering
philosophy** (deny by default, provider-verified identity, persistent scheduling, no silent
failures) · **Credits** (actual contributors only, supplied by the owner; no invented names and no
personal details without consent) · "Designed & Engineered by PDA BLISS".

## Create-appointment flow (implemented as an EP00 prototype)

1. **What**: title, category (6, each with icon and label), optional location.
2. **When**: native date and time pickers, Buddhist Era long-form confirmation, all-day switch
   (reminders anchor to 08:00).
3. **Who and where**: searchable destination selector that defaults to private-only. Each row
   shows kind, workspace, member count and the user's role. Viewer-only and pending destinations
   are visible but cannot be selected and show the reason. Warnings: group visibility, broad
   group, sensitive category in group or company group, mixed workspaces, cannot revoke.
   Minimal-disclosure switch.
4. **When to remind**: preset chips, at most 5. Actual fire times are listed; past, duplicate
   and over-limit reminders are explained.
5. **Review**: full summary, warnings, estimated quota, explicit note that saving and sending are
   separate steps. **Back** is always available.
6. **Confirm (simulated)**: states clearly that nothing was saved or sent.
